Vendored rather than loaded from a CDN. The CDN URL this replaced pointed at
/dist/qrcode.min.js, a path that does not exist in the package, so it answered
404 with a 73 byte error page. The card generator guarded the library with
"typeof qrcode !== 'undefined'" and skipped the QR step when it was missing,
which meant every card generated through the onboarding wizard and the bulk
generator was saved without a QR code and nobody was told.
