Cardify is a digital business card platform built in Muscat by BHD Group. It
issues one card per employee in Arabic and English with correct right-to-left
layout, a trackable QR code, and passes for both Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.
Staff keep their own details current through a self-service portal, so cards do
not go stale between print runs.

Twenty-four companies issue cards on the platform today. Platform access is
free with no expiry, no card required and no limit on employees or templates;
revenue comes from printing, which starts at
OMR 5.000 per 100 cards with delivery across Oman in two to four working days.
Cardify also publishes a free public directory of 2,502 Omani enterprises.
