# Segment 4: Companies and corporates

Angle: a concrete, small, free thing they can do this week. This is the lowest
hit-rate segment and the one most likely to be read as spam, so it gets the
tightest targeting and the shortest message.

Rules: only send where you can name something specific and true about them.
If the only thing you know is the company name, do not send. A generic info@
blast is what gets a domain blocked, and it is not worth the reply rate.

---

> **Do not type a sign-off.** Sending from `ali@bhd.om` makes the signature
> filter at :10024 append "Ali Darwish, Chief Executive Officer, +968 7161 6161"
> to every message. A name in the body produces two conflicting sign-offs. End
> the body at the last content sentence, then the opt-out and postal lines only.
> The filter does not add a postal address, so the line below stays (CAN-SPAM).

## Notes for the sender
- `{{specific_observation}}` is the whole message. One true, checked sentence:
  a recent hire announcement, a new branch, a rebrand, a careers page hiring 30
  people. If you cannot write it honestly, drop the contact.
- Outside Oman, cut the print pricing entirely. The platform is the pitch.
- Never send twice. This segment gets one message and no follow-up.
