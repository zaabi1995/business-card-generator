How theme detection works

1. Server renders card front template background to a canvas
2. Samples average brightness of the background color/image
3. Dark card (brightness < 128) → Light page theme with white/gray background
4. Light card (brightness ≥ 128) → Dark page theme with navy/charcoal background
5. Company's primary color used for accents (buttons, links, badges) in both modes
6. Fallback: If no generated card exists yet, use company theme's secondary color to decide
